HOW TO PLAY

Everything you need to know about Ebon Crucible's combat system, classes, and ranked play.

▼
THE ARENA
Ebon Crucible is a real-time 1v1 arena combat game set in a dark fantasy world. Two players enter the arena, each controlling a champion with unique abilities. The last one standing wins.
INSTANT PLAY
Play in your browser or on Steam. Jump straight into the arena — no setup, no waiting.
NO PAY-TO-WIN
Every fight is fair. No random elements, no grinding. Pure skill determines the outcome.
PURE SKILL
Ability usage, positioning, and decision-making. Every victory is earned.
COMBAT SYSTEM
Each class has 12+ unique abilities with cooldowns, cast times, and resource costs. Manage your resource while reading your opponent's cooldowns and positioning.
CC
Stuns, roots, silences, fears. Chain them to lock down opponents.
INTERRUPTS
Kick cast-time abilities to prevent healing or burst. Timing is everything.
DEFENSIVES
Shields, damage reduction, immunities, and healing to survive burst.
BURST
Stack cooldowns, CC the opponent, and unload. Knowing when to commit wins games.
POSITIONING
WASD movement. Kite melee, chase ranged, dodge abilities.
