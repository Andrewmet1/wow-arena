Ebon Crucible Alpha Launches

FEBRUARY 2026

The Ebon Crucible alpha is live. Five classes, 63 abilities, real-time 1v1 arena combat — all playable for free in your browser. No account required. Just pick a class and fight.

WAYS TO PLAY

RANKED 1V1 & 2V2
Climb the rating ladder from The Broken to The Ascended. Solo or duo queue. 3v3 coming soon.
WAGER DUELS
Challenge higher-rated players for coins. Winner takes all with amplified ELO stakes.
CUSTOM GAME
Challenge a friend with a 4-letter room code. Fight on your terms.
VS AI
Solo and team practice against AI at three difficulty levels. Learn matchups and combos.

SOCIAL FEATURES

Party up with friends for 2v2 ranked. Spectate live champion matches. Chat globally, in duel lobbies, or send direct messages. The Crucible is a community, not just an arena.

BUILT FOR THE BROWSER

Ebon Crucible runs entirely in your browser using WebGL. No plugins, no installs, no launcher. Works on any modern browser. The arena loads in seconds.

READY TO FIGHT?

Free to play. Browser or Steam (coming soon). Start fighting in seconds.
