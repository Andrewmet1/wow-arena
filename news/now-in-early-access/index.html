WHY EARLY ACCESS

The arena is real, the matches are real, and the ladder is live. Early Access means ranked competition is open today, and the rest of the roadmap will land in front of players, not behind a wall. Every match shapes balance. Every rank earned is permanent.

WHAT'S NEXT

  • Procedural Dungeon Crawl — Solo and co-op PvE runs, one dungeon per class, with room-by-room progression and roguelike upgrades.
  • 3v3 Ranked — A third arena format alongside 1v1 and 2v2, with full party ranked support.
  • New champions and skins — The roster expands. The cosmetic catalog expands faster.
  • Cross-platform play — Live now. PC, mobile, and web players already queue into the same matches.
