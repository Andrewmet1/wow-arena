Rotation Depth: Ability Synergies

FEBRUARY 19, 2026

All five classes receive new ability synergies, combo mechanics, and counterplay options in Patch 0.2. This update deepens the skill ceiling while keeping each class's identity intact.

TYRANT

Execute threshold raised. Armor Break now amplifies all physical damage. Reckless Fury resets Execute cooldown on kill. The Tyrant's endgame fantasy is now fully realized — break their armor, build rage, and finish them when they're wounded.

WRAITH

Shadowstrike from stealth now generates bonus Combo Points. Eviscerate scaling improved. New Smoke Bomb creates a zone that breaks targeting. The Wraith's opener-to-finisher pipeline is sharper than ever.

INFERNAL

Consuming 4 Cinder stacks now grants Ignition — your next fire spell becomes instant cast. Stack management now leads to explosive burst windows. Skilled Infernals will weave spells to build stacks, then unleash devastating instant-cast combos.

HARBINGER

Soul Shard generation from DoT ticks increased. Haunt now amplifies all damage on the target. Fear duration reduced but now breaks on less damage. The Harbinger's attrition game is stronger, with clearer windows to capitalize on shard generation.

REVENANT

Holy Power spenders buffed across the board. Consecration now generates Holy Power passively. Divine Shield cooldown reduced. The Revenant's hybrid playstyle rewards players who balance offense and defense through smart Holy Power management.
