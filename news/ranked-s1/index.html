Ranked Season 1 Now Live

FEBRUARY 2026

Ranked matchmaking has arrived in Ebon Crucible. Compete against players of similar skill level, climb the rating ladder, and earn your place among the most feared combatants in the arena.

HOW IT WORKS

Every player starts at 1500 rating. Win matches to gain rating, lose to drop. The amount gained or lost depends on the skill difference between you and your opponent. Beat someone above your rank and you'll climb fast. Lose to someone below and it hurts.

KING OF THE HILL

The highest-rated player on the leaderboard is crowned the Crucible Champion. This title is displayed on the leaderboard and announced to all players when the crown changes hands. Every match at the top is a battle to hold or seize the throne.

2V2 RANKED — NOW LIVE

Team arena is here. Queue solo for a random partner or party up with a friend for ranked 2v2. Separate ELO rating, same five tiers. Coordinate cooldowns, chain CC, and execute team combos. 3v3 coming soon.

SPECTATE LIVE MATCHES

Watch the top players compete in real time. When a class champion is in an active match, a LIVE indicator appears on the Rankings tab. Click to spectate and study the best.

BATTLE PASS — SEASON 1

50 tiers of exclusive cosmetic rewards. Earn season XP from every ranked match — 1v1 and 2v2. Unlock portrait frames, portraits, and titles as you climb. All rewards are visual-only. Flex your dedication, not your wallet.

READY TO FIGHT?

Free to play. Browser or Steam (coming soon). Start fighting in seconds.
