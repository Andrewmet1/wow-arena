CHAMPION BETTING

Every class has a champion — the highest-rated player on that class. Now you can put your coins where your conviction is.

Place a bet on whether the current champion holds their position or falls within the next 5 ranked matches. Odds are based on the champion's win rate — betting against a dominant champion pays big, but is risky.

HOLDS
Champion survives 5 matches. Lower odds, safer bet.
1.15x – 1.5x payout
FALLS
Champion is dethroned. Higher odds, bigger payoff.
1.8x – 4.5x payout

HOW THE POOL WORKS

Losing bets aren't burned — they fund the winners. When bets resolve, the losing side's coins pay the winning side's payouts first. Then the surplus gets split:

STEP 1
Losers' coins pay winners at their fixed odds
STEP 2
Champion earns 15% of the surplus
STEP 3
Rest flows into the Crucible Jackpot

THE CHAMPION'S CUT

When people bet falls on you and you hold your throne, you don't just win glory — you get 15% of the surplus after winners are paid.

More people betting against you means more coins in your pocket when you prove them wrong. It's a reward for staying on top under pressure — and it makes being champion feel like something worth defending.

THE CRUCIBLE JACKPOT

After winners and champions are paid, every remaining coin flows into the Crucible Jackpot — a community prize pool that grows with every resolved bet.

The jackpot will fund tournaments, weekly challenges, seasonal events, and special rewards. The more the community bets, the bigger the prizes get for everyone.

EXAMPLE
10 players bet 500 coins each on the Tyrant champion falling (5,000 pool). 5 players bet 200 each on holds (1,000 pool). Champion holds — "holds" winners get 1,300 total (1.3x odds). From the 5,000 loser pool: 1,300 pays winners, the champion earns 555 coins (15% of 3,700 surplus), and 3,145 coins flow into the Crucible Jackpot.

WEEKLY CHALLENGES

Every week, the Crucible generates new challenges funded by the Jackpot. An AI Game Director analyzes player activity, class pick rates, and game health to create tailored objectives.

CHALLENGE TYPES
Class wins, win streaks, class variety, underdog victories, and more
REWARDS
50–200 coins per challenge, drawn from the Jackpot

Challenges rotate every Sunday. If a class is underplayed, expect challenges that reward exploring it. If activity is strong and balanced, expect fun variety objectives. The system adapts to keep the Crucible healthy.
