WAGER DUELS

Think you're better than the player above you on the ladder? Prove it. Wager Duels let you challenge any higher-rated player for Crucible Coins — with amplified ELO stakes that make every match count double, triple, or more.

This isn't matchmaking. This is personal. You pick your opponent, set your wager, and choose your amplifier. The higher the stakes, the bigger the ELO swing. Climb the ladder your way.

HOW IT WORKS
1
Challenge a higher-rated player from the Rankings tab
Choose your wager amount (50–500 coins) and ELO amplifier (2x–5x)
2
They have 48 hours to accept or decline
Coins are held in escrow — safe until the duel resolves
3
Once accepted, both players have 24 hours to ready up
Both click Ready — and the match begins immediately
4
Winner takes the wager. ELO change amplified by your chosen tier
A 5x amplifier on a 500-coin wager can swing 80+ ELO in a single match

SAFETY NET
No-shows don't cost you. If your opponent doesn't accept within 48 hours, or either player fails to ready up within 24 hours, the duel expires and all coins are refunded. No penalty, no risk.

CHANNEL CHAT

The Crucible now has a full chat system. Talk strategy in Global, trash-talk your opponent in Duel Chat, or message friends directly with DMs.

GLOBAL
Public chat visible to all online players. IRC-style timestamps.
DUEL
Private channel between opponents during a match. Auto-created, auto-cleaned.
DM
Private messages with friends. Persistent history, toast notifications.

The chat panel sits at the bottom-left of your screen with tabs for each channel. Minimize it during matches, expand it between fights. Unread badges show you what you missed. Game keys are automatically suppressed while typing — no accidental ability casts mid-sentence.

👑

WHY IT MATTERS

Ranked matchmaking finds you a fair fight. Wager Duels let you pick your fight. See someone sitting at the top with a class you think you can beat? Challenge them. Put your coins and your rating on the line.

Combined with global chat, the Crucible is no longer just a place to queue and fight. It's a community where rivalries form, reputations build, and every duel has a story.
