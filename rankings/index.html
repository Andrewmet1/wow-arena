HOW RANKED WORKS

01
QUEUE UP
Select your class and enter the ranked queue. Our matchmaking system pairs you against opponents of similar skill rating for fair, competitive fights.
02
WIN TO CLIMB
Victory earns rating. Defeat costs it. Your tier reflects your current rating in real time — there is no rank decay or placement matches. Pure performance.
03
SEIZE THE CROWN
Reach the top of the ladder to claim the title of Crucible Champion. The entire server is notified when the crown changes hands.
